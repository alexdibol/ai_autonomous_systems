# Autonomous Systems Notebook Sandbox

This directory contains the **50 Jupyter notebooks** that operationalize the ideas developed in the 13 papers under [`/readings`](../readings/).

## How to use the collection

Do not treat the papers and notebooks as separate resources. Read the paper first to acquire the conceptual model; then open the corresponding notebook below and work through its cells in order. Where a paper maps to a complete lab, begin with the linked starting notebook and continue numerically through the remaining notebooks in that lab.

The notebook prefix is intentional:

- `LAB##` identifies the laboratory in the nine-lab sandbox.
- `NB##` identifies the notebook's position inside that laboratory.
- The remaining filename describes the experiment or governed workflow.
