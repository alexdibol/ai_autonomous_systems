# Autonomous Systems Sandbox

## A guided map from concepts to monographs to executable laboratories

This repository is a pedagogical environment for understanding autonomous systems by moving through three connected layers:

1. **Readings** — short conceptual papers that introduce the ideas, architectures, protocols, and governance questions.
2. **Laboratory papers** — nine integrated monographs that organize those ideas into complete experimental settings.
3. **Jupyter notebooks** — 50 executable laboratories that make the arguments observable, testable, and operational.

The three collections are deliberately **many-to-many**. A foundational reading may support several laboratories, while a single laboratory may synthesize several readings. The map below identifies the strongest pedagogical connections.

> Begin with the [main paper: *Autonomous Systems Sandbox*](MAIN%20PAPER%20AUTONOMOUS%20SYSTEMS%20SANDBOX.pdf) for the unifying narrative of the complete collection.

## How to study the collection

For each laboratory:

1. **Read the conceptual papers** to acquire the vocabulary and understand the central problem.
2. **Study the laboratory paper** to see how the concepts are assembled into a coherent architecture.
3. **Run the notebooks in numerical order** to observe the architecture being constructed one controlled step at a time.
4. **Compare the notebook outputs with the monograph's claims**: identify what is automated, what is remembered, what remains human-controlled, and where governance enters the loop.

The notebooks are not appendices to the papers. They are the experimental counterpart of the argument. The papers explain *why* the architecture matters; the notebooks reveal *how* its components behave.

## The overall learning progression

- **Labs 01–03:** build the conceptual and knowledge foundations—second brains, governed memory, vaults, retrieval, and durable context.
- **Labs 04–06:** move from model intelligence to reasoning, tool use, MCP-mediated infrastructure, and governed autonomous workflows.
- **Labs 07–09:** integrate those components into institutional exobrains for tax planning, civil litigation, and investment banking.

The collection therefore moves from **understanding autonomous systems**, to **disassembling their components**, to **governing their operation**, and finally to **creating domain-specific autonomous intelligence**.
